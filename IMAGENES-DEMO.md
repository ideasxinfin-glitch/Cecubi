## Prompts para crear las imágenes

Uniformes reales (para que coincidan con las fotos del colegio):
- **Maternal y preescolar:** playera deportiva con degradado naranja a blanco, cuello y puños azul marino, short azul marino con franja naranja.
- **Primaria y secundaria:** playera deportiva azul marino lisa, pantalón o short azul marino.

No pidas el logotipo en la ropa: la IA lo deforma. Se ve más natural sin logo.

Base para todos los prompts (pegar al final): `Natural editorial photography, warm sunlight, Mexican school in Mérida Yucatán, real children with natural expressions, no text, no logos, no watermarks, shallow depth of field, high resolution.`

1. **hero.jpg** — `Three smiling Mexican elementary school kids (ages 7-9) hugging side by side, looking at the camera, navy blue sports t-shirts, outdoor school patio with green artificial grass and colorful fence softly blurred behind, subjects placed on the right two-thirds of the frame, empty soft space on the left, horizontal 3:2.`
2. **por-que.jpg** — `Classroom of Mexican primary students raising their hands enthusiastically, seen from the side, navy blue t-shirts, bright classroom, wide panoramic 8:3 framing, right half slightly darker and less busy for text overlay.`
3. **vida-1.jpg** — `Mexican boy about 8 years old holding a basketball on an outdoor school court, navy blue sports t-shirt, smiling, vertical 2:3.`
4. **vida-2.jpg** — `Group of four smiling Mexican schoolgirls (ages 8-10) posing together in a school courtyard, navy blue t-shirts, horizontal 3:2.`
5. **vida-3.jpg** — `Mexican preschool boy concentrating on a craft with colored paper and glue at a small green table, orange and white sports t-shirt with navy collar, horizontal 3:2.`
6. **vida-4.jpg** — `Female teacher kneeling beside two Mexican preschool children working on a puzzle at a table, warm and caring moment, vertical 5:7.`
7. **vida-5.jpg** — `Mexican primary students sitting in a circle on the floor of a bright classroom talking with their teacher, community and values activity, vertical 5:7.`
8. **admisiones.jpg** — `Smiling Mexican boy about 7 years old wearing a backpack, standing at a white school entrance with orange columns, navy blue t-shirt, subject on the left half, plain darker background on the right, vertical 2:3.`

Para las instalaciones (aulas, biblioteca, cancha) usa solo fotos reales: inventarlas mostraría espacios que los papás no van a encontrar en la visita.
